# Privacy Policy — روحانی ڈھال (Roohani Dhaal)
Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Developer: مفتی محمد سہیل البازی · Contact: **[اپنا ای میل یہاں لکھیں / insert your contact email]**

## Summary
Roohani Dhaal works fully offline. We do not require an account, and we do not collect, store on a server, or sell your personal information. Duas, search, favourites and your daily checklist stay on your device.

## Data stored on your device only
Favourite duas, today's "read" marks, font-size and dark-mode settings. This data never leaves your phone through our app and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). When ads load, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data such as the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction/diagnostic data, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads. Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Ads settings: https://myadcenter.google.com
Ads appear in a separate strip below the content and are not part of any dua or hadith.

## Permissions
`INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` — used only by the ads SDK. No camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or phone permissions.

## Sharing
When you tap "Share", the text of the dua is handed to the app you choose (e.g. WhatsApp). We do not see or receive it.

## Children
The app is a general-audience religious reference and is not directed at children under 13. If you publish it under Play's Families programme, ad settings must be reviewed first.

## Changes and contact
We may update this policy; the date above will change. Questions: the contact above.
